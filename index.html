Why do we learn?

I like to learn. I think most people do. "Learning" doesn't need to be about information. Not everything is data

It could be about learning to move your body or developing instincts for different skills.

It could also mean learning about yourself and other people, and the world. Ultimately, this is what all learning is.

The things we can learn about change through time

But some things recur:

  • Art
  • Music
  • Sport and Games


Maybe this is something special about Earth, or maybe space dust learns too.

There are many scientific reasons. Learning keeps us alive. But what drives that impulse: for life to continue? At the end of the day, science can't really tell us.

Learning, like living, is just what we do.
